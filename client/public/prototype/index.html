Process

How we actually work

A fixed-price, documented process from audit to handoff. No retainers, no lock-in, no black boxes.

  1. Discovery & audit

    We map your current workflow, identify where manual work is eating your team's time, and find every automation opportunity. No guesswork — a clear picture of what's worth building first.

  2. Scope & fixed quote

    You get a clear scope document and a fixed price before we write a single line of code. No hourly surprises, no scope creep billed back to you.

  3. Build & weekly check-ins

    We build in your actual environment with weekly updates, so you always know where things stand.

  4. Test & harden

    Every integration is tested against failure states, bad payloads, network timeouts and duplicate events — the same QA discipline used in regulated enterprise systems.

  5. Handoff & documentation

    You receive webhook endpoints, trigger conditions, field mappings and operational runbooks. A system your team can understand and maintain — not a black box.
